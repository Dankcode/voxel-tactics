# Gameplay sound effects

These five original Mixkit files were supplied by the user on 2026-10-15 and copied
without changing their audio. No additional audio was downloaded.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| mixkit-crunchy-footsteps-loop-535.wav | Continuous walking, stopped during vaults/climbing and at the destination |
| mixkit-explosion-hit-1704.wav | Nearby grenade, breach and other explosion events |
| mixkit-epic-impact-afar-explosion-2782.wav | Explosions more than 18 map units from the tactical camera focus |
| mixkit-shotgun-pump-1659.wav | Combat Shotgun cycling after a shot and on reload |
| mixkit-handgun-click-1660.mp3 | Sidearm readying and reloading |

The weapon clips are mechanical handling sounds, not gunshot substitutes.
Extinguisher bursts do not play an explosion. Hidden enemies and mechanical walkers
do not use the human footsteps. Settings → Audio controls the saved effects volume;
zero mutes everything. Playback starts after a pointer/key gesture and stops when
leaving battle or hiding the browser tab.
