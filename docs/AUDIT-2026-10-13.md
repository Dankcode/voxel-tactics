## Findings

### 1. P1 — Floor picking rejects stairs, ramps and intermediate terrain heights

`client/src/Game.js:613` supplies `preferredFloor: top - 3` whenever the cut is finite, including automatic zoom cuts. `client/src/render/TilePicking.js:26` rejects every fallback hit whose standing height differs from that exact floor. Visible, walkable stair steps and terrain contours at intermediate heights therefore cannot be selected, even when they are below the cut. This affects the very elevation routes introduced today.

Reproduction: a concrete floor at z1 with a stone stair at `(5,5,z2)`, a vertical ray through that cell, and cutZ 4. Without preferredFloor, picking returns `{x:5,y:5,z:2}`. With Game's preferredFloor 1, it returns null. Allow intermediate traversable surfaces belonging to the displayed floor band; do not use exact height equality as the fallback filter.

### 2. P2 — Interior hold-zone destruction checks bypass indoor cover

`shared/mapgen/district/HoldZone.js:141` excludes every voxel inside a building from soft-cover removal, including crates and furniture. At line 151 the grenade target score likewise ignores all cover inside buildings. For a zone sufficiently far inside a building, every score is zero and line 154 exits before applying even one grenade. The blast gate can consequently pass without destruction being simulated.

Reproduction: a 26 × 26 shop at `(6,6)` with a 7 × 7 interior zone `(15,15)`–`(21,21)` and an indoor crate at `(18,18,z2)`. Capture the two stores returned by clone during evaluation: the soft-cover clone retains the crate, the blast clone has the same hash as the original, and `gates.blast` is true. This fixture's overall hold result fails other checks; the false-positive blast result is independent of those failures. Classify cover by material/structural role rather than excluding the entire building footprint.

### 3. P2 — Road-clearance gate skips building footprints

`shared/mapgen/DistrictGates.js:42` skips any cell within a building before evaluating the road air column. A building intersecting a road is therefore invisible to the gate intended to prevent that violation. If road tags are subsequently overwritten as building tags, the gate also loses the original road ownership.

Reproduction: register a horizontal road over rows 18–22 and build a 14 × 14 office at `(8,14)`, crossing the road. `gates.roadAir` remains true. The fixture's land-use gate fails because the remaining empty ground is deliberately undressed; roadAir still independently reports the wrong result. Preserve road ownership separately and evaluate its air column even inside building bounds.

### 4. P2 — Traffic None is overridden by cover repair

`shared/mapgen/district/District.js:55` invokes repairCover without traffic settings. `shared/mapgen/district/CoverRepair.js:97`–100 then creates abandoned cars directly, bypassing placeVehicles' traffic-none check. The result contradicts the lab control and makes collision experiments inconsistent.

Reproduction at seed 27 with `{traffic:'none', pedestrian:'none'}`: Park has 7 vehicles, Market has 5, and Drainage has 4. Hillside has 0 for this seed. Pass the traffic choice into repair and use a non-vehicle cover treatment when traffic is disabled. Repair vehicles are also absent from the report's streetObjects list, which is assembled from the earlier vehicle pass.

## Follow-up: building on Claude's implementation

After Leon asked to learn from Claude's repository and build on it, the shared skeleton → parcel mixes → dressing → validation pipeline was retained and extended:

- Floor-band picking now accepts intermediate stair/ramp tops; visible raised surfaces take precedence over the nominal floor plane behind them. Soldier selection uses the same height band.
- Indoor soft furniture is removed in the durability clone, and indoor cover participates in grenade scoring. Hold reports now expose removed-soft-cover counts and actual blast centres/destruction counts.
- Separate road ownership retains road datums even after building tags or ground snapshots change. The gate inspects building intersections and preserves explicitly authored upper crossings with lower clearance. Authored crossing rails/checkpoint barriers have explicit road-set origins.
- Traffic None reaches cover repair, which uses barriers instead of abandoned cars. All placed vehicles, including repair vehicles, appear in debug metadata.
- District finalization now rejects failed gates by default, with map/seed diagnostics and the complete gate report on the error. Explicit report mode is available for diagnostic callers.
- `npm run mapgen:audit -- --determinism` provides a repeatable seven-map, four-seed check, optional full-snapshot determinism comparison, JSON evidence, configuration overrides, and nonzero exit on failure.
