# 10 · AI

One tactical AI (`shared/battle/Ai.js`) plays the Syndicate, the squad autopilot (`?auto=1`, debug *Autoplay*) and
both sides of the balance sim (`tools/sim/battle_sim.mjs`). It only produces intents; the battle validates and
applies them like a player's.

## Who runs it

| Context | Driver | Per unit | Policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local enemy phase | `Game.maybeRunAi` → `decide` | up to 6 intents, until `decide` returns null or an intent is rejected | `{ formations: false }` |
| Local squad autopilot | `Game.maybeRunAi` → `decide` | same | shared `formationIntents` planner before each squad phase |
| Online enemy phase | `MissionRoom.runEnemyTurn` → `decide` (server) | same | default |
| Balance sim | `Ai.runAiSide` (max 80 actions per side) | up to 6 | squad `{ formations }` from `--formations-compare`; enemy default |

`DEFAULT_POLICY` = `{ formations: false, breach: true, grenadeMinTargets: 2, shootThreshold: 0.9, samples: 24 }`.
Units act in roster order; each unit finishes before the next starts. Then the side ends its turn.

## `decide(battle, unit)`: one intent at a time

1. **Idle**: an unalerted enemy does nothing (there is no patrol). A unit with no AP and no move-only AP stops.
2. **Knowledge**: "known" enemies are those the unit's whole side can see (`visibleTo`). When nothing is known, units
   still head for the **nearest enemy anywhere on the map** (the AI is omniscient about positions for movement), or,
   for the squad, for the objective goal when there is one.
3. **Objective** (squad side only, ≥ 1 AP): `extract` when the unit is in an open extraction zone and the objective is
   `extract` or the unit carries the `recover` item; `recover` when the item is free and within Chebyshev 1 and
   |Δz| ≤ 1. The goal (`Objectives.objectiveGoal`) is the item while it lies free, else the zone centre for
   `extract`, `hold`, and `recover` once the item is carried; none for `eliminate`. The enemy AI ignores objectives.
4. **Melee-only units** (the Enforcer): hit an adjacent known enemy if AP allows; otherwise move toward the nearest
   known (else any) enemy, keeping the melee AP (`approach`).
5. **Breach** (squad only, `policy.breach`, ≥ 2 AP): if a breach point leads to an unrevealed room, breach with every
   squad member within 2 tiles that has ≥ 1 AP; entry is `charge` when someone carries one and no enemy is known,
   else `door`.
6. **Ammo**: reload if the primary ranged weapon can't fire; switch to the primary ranged weapon if another is
   selected.
7. **Grenades**: for each damaging, non-placed throwable it can afford, score each known enemy within range:
   enemies within the radius + 0.6 if that enemy has FULL cover from the thrower − 3 × allies within radius + 0.5.
   Throw at the best target scoring ≥ `grenadeMinTargets` − 0.5 (1.5). Smoke and Extinguisher grenades are never
   used.
8. **Shot value** (`bestShot`): for each known enemy within max range, torso and head:
   `hit × projectiles × max(0, damage − max(0, armour − pierce)) ÷ AP − 10 × friendly`, ×1.5 when it likely kills.
   Discarded below 0.05. Uses the same `estimateShot` as the HUD (24 samples).
9. **Shoot now** if there is a shot and (the unit can't both move 1 AP and shoot, or the value ≥ 2 × `shootThreshold`
   (1.8), or it has already moved this turn).
10. **Formation members** (not leaders, not Crossfire) hold their slot: shoot if possible, else overwatch toward the
   nearest known enemy.
11. **Nothing known**: advance along the real path toward the objective goal if there is one (squad), else the
    nearest enemy, as far as AP − 1 allows (stops after
    moving once with < 2 AP left, or after 12+ steps this turn).
12. **Reposition** once per turn (`bestTile`), keeping the shot's AP: then shoot; else overwatch toward the nearest
    known enemy; else reload.

The AI never uses abilities, doors, melee (unless melee-only), smoke, extinguishers or charges outside a squad
breach.

## Tile scoring (`bestTile`)

Over the tiles reachable with (AP + move-only AP − shot AP, min 1) × tiles per AP, sampled down to about 260:

| Term | Score |
|---|---|
| cover against each threat within 30 tiles (`coverAgainst`) | FULL +14 · HALF +8 · NONE −9 |
| the threat would be flanked from here, within weapon range | +10 per threat |
| distance to the nearest threat vs 0.7 × weapon range | −0.7 per tile of difference |
| burning surface or burning voxel | −40 |
| allies within Chebyshev 2 | −4 each |
| height above the lowest threat | +2 per voxel |
| objective goal (squad only): distance to it | −2.2 per tile for `extract` or the item carrier, −1.2 for `hold`, −0.8 otherwise |
| objective zone (squad only): tile inside it, kind ≠ `recover` | +25 |
| among the top 12: can see a threat (eye at z + 2.4 to its chest) within max range | +16 |

Highest total wins. Threats are the known enemies, or every enemy when none is known.

## Squad formations in the sim (`formationIntents`, `policy.formations`)

Both `runAiSide` and client autoplay call the pure `formationIntents` planner and submit its ordered intents. At the start of the side: break Shield Lines when no enemy is known. Otherwise, for each unit with ≥ 2 AP that has
cover toward the centroid of the known enemies, try a Shield Line facing that centroid with up to 2 nearby
(Chebyshev ≤ 3) unformed mates, only if every slot also has cover toward it.
